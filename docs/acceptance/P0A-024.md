- Comparison & Dual Digital-Twin module (`packages/desktop_worker/twin_comparison.py`):
  - `compute_dual_digital_twin_metrics(...)` reports (1) `design_pass_fail_probability_pct` from modeled limits and Monte Carlo `PCG64` margin UQ (`0.0%` / `FAIL` on any hard limit breach; `None` / `UNVERIFIED_ESTIMATE` when unverified parameters exist), and (2) `physical_evidence_match_pct` (`None` / `UNVALIDATED` without aligned source measurements; bounded comparison score when matched).
