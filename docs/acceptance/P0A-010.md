## 4. Physical root causes (per-window evidence, re-derivable from the pinned recordings via the frozen extract/score functions)

Diagnosis statistics below are computed over the eligible windows BEFORE the
frozen median-RPM-support filter (the frozen scoring additionally drops
0–2 windows per flight; the frozen counts above come from the committed
artifacts).

**(a) FAULT_M3_10 vibration miss — weak fault-to-IMU transfer + autopilot
compensation + non-attributing score.** The faulted motor (ESC_2) was spun UP
by the autopilot (median 6,079 RPM vs 5,362 for the other five — +13 %),
shifting its order-1 frequency to ~101 Hz. The rotor-order score takes the
MAXIMUM normalized amplitude over ALL motors' ±4 Hz neighborhoods and cannot
attribute energy to a motor; the faulted motor's own 1P bin carried only a
median 0.21 of the winning-bin power. The entire flight's score distribution
(min/median/max 0.040/0.066/0.0987) lies BELOW the healthy-calibration
threshold (0.1034) — the 1P-band amplitude at the IMU never rose above the
healthy spread for this arm/damage combination.

**(b) FAULT_M1_10 vibration miss — same mechanism, plus fragmented
exceedances.** Faulted motor (ESC_0) compensated to 6,557 RPM vs 5,471 others
(+20 %; its ~109 Hz 1P is near the top of the 55–135 Hz scan band); own-1P
power ratio median 0.11; the winning bins were other motors' (75–77 Hz).
Scores max 0.1137 with only 3 exceedances, never adjacent (max run 1).

**(c) FAULT_M1_10 electrical miss — a REAL sustained deficit defeated by the
adjacency rule.** The faulted ESC's deficit was genuine and persistent:
median 0.0814 across 38 windows, exceeding the 0.1067 threshold in windows
10, 16, 18 and 27 (scores 0.108–0.1235) — all on the SAME ESC (ESC_0) but
never in two ADJACENT windows, so the frozen two-consecutive-window rule
never fired. (Raw power: 0 exceedances — the deficit only exists after
RPM normalization.)

**(d) FAULT_M5_10 electrical miss — the electrical signature is genuinely
small; the observable moved in vibration.** The faulted ESC's deficit was
tiny (median 0.0263, max 0.0920 < 0.1067, leading the deficit in only 3
windows); its speed-normalized power (55.44) sat near mid-pack against its
healthy center (53.85). The window-level load redistribution moved the
largest deficits to OTHER ESCs (ESC_2/ESC_3: 0.133–0.181). The vibration
channel carried the entire signature (36/36 exceedances, run 26) — the
imbalance observable and the electrical observable are complementary on this
rig, not redundant.

**(e) NO_FAULT6 healthy ESC false alert — stationary per-motor load
asymmetry, re-flagged by the across-ESC re-normalization.** ESC_2 is the
inherently low-load motor: its deficit was ALREADY the largest of all six
ESCs inside the healthy calibration flights (NO_FAULT1 median 0.0717 max
0.1067; NO_FAULT4 median 0.0651 max 0.1307 — the latter already exceeds the
threshold). In NO_FAULT6 the same motor again ranked most deficient (median
0.0879, max 0.1958) and finally produced two adjacent exceedances. The
per-flight per-ESC power medians are non-stationary (e.g., ESC_0: 51.43 here
vs 56.43 healthy center; ESC_1: 54.58 vs 51.94) — a window-internal
re-normalization cannot distinguish "the always-weak motor" from "a newly
weakened motor" without a per-motor self-baseline.

**(f) NO_FAULT5 accel-RMS false alert — flight-dynamics load confounding.**
The RMS feature measures total detrended acceleration variance (manoeuvre +
turbulence + vibration). The healthy holdout flight's OWN RMS tail sits at
the threshold: its p95 is 0.624 vs the threshold 0.5876 (calibrated as the
95th percentile of only 41 pooled TRAIN windows). Windows 54–55 (median RPM
5,633/5,489 — mid-range, no anomaly in RPM or rotor-order score 0.054/0.064)
exceeded twice in a row — a manoeuvre/load excursion of a healthy flight,
with no fault-specific content. The same feature missed both faults (M3: 2
isolated; M4: 4 isolated) — it is load-confounded in both directions.

## 5. Repair specification for P0A-011

1. **Per-motor self-baseline for the electrical channel** (fixes FP-e and
   sharpens c/d): compare each ESC's speed-normalized power against ITS OWN
   healthy per-window distribution (per-ESC residual/quantile), keeping the
   across-ESC re-normalization only as a secondary channel. The ESC_2
   asymmetry is stationary and must be calibrated out, not re-detected every
   flight.
2. **Two-sided electrical excursion** (fixes d): score both tails of the
   per-motor residual — drag-type damage raises the faulted ESC's normalized
   load (M5's ESC_4 sat mid-pack while its vibration signature was maximal);
   the one-sided deficit rule is structurally blind to power-increasing
   faults.
3. **Persistence rule on one channel** (fixes c): replace "two ADJACENT
   windows led by the same ESC" with a persistence rule tolerant to gaps —
   e.g., the same ESC exceeding in ≥ k of the last W windows (M1: 4
   exceedances of 38 windows on one ESC = a persistent deficit), or a CUSUM
   on the per-ESC residual series. Adjacency must not be the only persistence
   test.
4. **Per-motor vibration attribution** (fixes a/b): score each motor's OWN
   order-1 bin against its own healthy band instead of the max over all
   motors' neighborhoods (the winning bin was the faulted motor's in a
   minority of windows; own-1P/ winning-bin power median 0.02–0.21 even in
   DETECTED flights — today's alert presence is governed by aggregate band
   energy, not by the fault signature). Track the faulted motors' RPM
   compensation (observed +13 %/+20 %) as a supporting feature.
5. **Drop or manoeuvre-gate the accel-RMS baseline** (fixes f): it measures
   flight dynamics, not faults; if retained, normalize against the
   manoeuvre/RPM-matched healthy band per window rather than a global
   percentile.
6. **Threshold policy**: with only two healthy calibration recordings (41
   pooled windows), per-motor quantiles are statistically thin — predeclare
   the M25 tolerance policy (`docs/VALIDATION_TOLERANCES.md`: fault-miss
   one-sided 95 % upper ≤ 0.01, healthy false-alarm ≤ 0.05 with the
   registered example counts) as the prospective acceptance gate and state
   that the DEVELOPMENT data cannot estimate those probabilities. The M25.11
   evidence boundary stands: one rig, retrospective, offline indicators.
