## Analytic and electrical references

The rotor oracle evaluates thrust along body +Z, moments
`[sum(y_i*T_i), -sum(x_i*T_i), -k*sum(spin_i*T_i)]`, and verifies both
unequal thrust and a zeroed branch. The rigid-body oracle uses a known
90-degree rotation and non-diagonal inertia to evaluate
`m*dv/dt = R*F + m*g`, `I*domega/dt + omega x (I*omega) = torque`,
and mechanical power `v dot F_total + omega dot torque`. The axial
fixture checks signed `T = rho*n^2*D^4*CT` and
`P = rho*n^3*D^5*CP = 2*pi*n*Q`. Separately, the sourced static
powertrain branch satisfies `P_electrical = V*I`. Shaft power and
bus power remain distinct. Static trimmed hover and drag-free ballistic
motion are integrated for 0.5 s and compared to exact trajectories.

Charge uses the sum of held branch currents over the published electrical
intervals and compares every cumulative value to the SOC decrement.
The discrete battery energy oracle evaluates OCV from tick-start SOC:
`E_ocv = sum(U_oc*I*dt)`, `E_loss = sum(R*I^2*dt)`, and
`E_bus,exact = sum((U_oc-I*R)*I*dt)`. Its algebra closes to roundoff.
The runner logs a numerically converged bus, so its measured difference
from the ideal algebra is separately checked against the integrated
fixed-point voltage tolerance. Bus energy also matches the sum of logged
branch electrical energies and wire losses within the wiring solve budget.
The partial thermal-stop case charges exactly **0.004 + 0.002 s** by
the event clock. The rigid state remains at the last completed 0.004 s
boundary. This is the discrete model balance, with no claim about chemical,
thermal or aerodynamic energy outside the implemented equations.

## Defects found and exact scope of fixes

1. **Thermal dwell clock drift.** In the approved base, adding 0.004 s
   nineteen times produced 0.07600000000000004 s although the tick boundary
   was 0.076 s. The deadline 0.07600000000000001 s fired prematurely at
   0.07599999999999998 s. In
   [runner.py](../../packages/physics/scenarios/runner.py), record the
   timestamp where above-rating current starts, compare its absolute
   deadline to the authoritative tick end, and emit the deadline itself.
   Direct variable-step diagnostics use a compensated local clock.
   Current equal to the rating disarms the exposure. Dwell equal to the
   declared duration fails. Adjacent representable limits on both sides
   now classify correctly.
2. **Nonfinite quaternion ingress.** In the approved base, normalizing
   `[NaN, 0, 0, 1]` returned four NaNs and let the invalid orientation
   enter state/matrix construction.
   [quaternion.py](../../packages/physics/dynamics/quaternion.py) now raises
   before division if the norm is nonfinite. The already strict external
   finite and unit checks remain in place.

Strict max tests cover current, voltage, RPM and thrust. The lower-voltage
limit reverses the comparison. Equality is admitted for instantaneous
max/min limits. Unknown winding drag, blade breakup and reallocation
refuse at the first eligible tick on either side of the boundary with
`INCONCLUSIVE`, no unsupported command and no recovery. Previous campaign
JSONs and P0A-005 through P0A-014 receipts are preserved.
