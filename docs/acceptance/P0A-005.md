Root-cause clues for P0A-006 (from the campaign docs and this reproduction):

- Both electrical models **overestimate mean voltage** and net delivered
  energy while the module discharges, so the modeled module voltage never
  reaches the sampled 80.0 V floor in any held-out cycle — the sag response
  under sustained high current is under-modeled by the fixed-R / one-RC
  forms fitted on early cycles.
- The dynamic (one-RC) model improves voltage MAE but not the cutoff
  prediction: the error is in the low-voltage tail, not the average.
- Voltage scores beyond the largest calibrated cumulative charge are
  withheld, so the end-of-discharge behavior was never calibrated — the
  cutoffs occur exactly in the uncalibrated region.
- The empirical lower band warned before every recorded crossing (median 8 s
  and 6 s early), which suggests the timing information exists in the model
  state and the deficit is in the absolute low-voltage tail accuracy.

## Honest scope

Independent published measurements on one repeatedly cycled 24S module,
retrospective chronological split. This calibration cannot support aged-pack
acceptance, prospective current demand, mission-endurance estimates or
flight release, and the sampled times cannot reconstruct equipment reaction
time. Independent replicates, a calibrated uncertainty budget and reviewer
acceptance remain required for M21 (see docs/M21_BATTERY_CAMPAIGN.md).
