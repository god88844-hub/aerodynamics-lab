**Tracking collapse miss.** For this diagnosis, the collapse window starts at
the first measured altitude below the 0–0.5 s hover median after the
post-baseline altitude peak. The median was 0.703516536 m and the peak
was 1.772749953 m at 1.9700 s. At 2.5575 s the aircraft was down to
0.677647762 m while the conditional model predicted 1.703400118 m.
It fell below 0.1 m at 2.9575 s (0.090934106 m measured and
1.703513684 m predicted). Through the final row at 7.0000 s, the
collapse window contains 1,778 samples. Model altitude MAE in that window was
**1.659103168 m**, with **1.703516535 m** maximum error and a **1.702789158 m**
overprediction at the final sample. The missed collapse is frozen as an
observed regression. The window definition is a diagnostic, separate from
flight acceptance tolerances. Measured voltage was 14.392382812 V at
the window start, already back above the study line. In the frozen code,
altitude is calculated from the baseline and filtered reference alone.
The separate sag prediction never limits the altitude response, so a
correct first crossing cannot change the modeled trajectory.

## Study and channel boundaries

- **14 V is a study-specific threshold for this QDrone benchmark.** Its
  historical scoring reports the first exported sample strictly below the
  line. Universal vehicle and battery limits come from applicable component
  evidence. These paired exports contain neither a calibrated current
  channel nor controller protection flags and firmware trigger timestamps.
  The voltage crossing and later altitude loss do not identify the
  shutdown mechanism or its trigger time.
- **Logged auxiliary reference `zauxd (m)` and drive commands `m1 (V)`
  through `m4 (V)` are measured inputs.** The altitude model uses the
  battery-aware auxiliary reference recorded in flight or the nominal
  reference `zd (m)` for original MPC. Voltage prediction uses the logged
  drive-command proxy. Both are conditional replay, including initialization
  from measured baseline altitude and loaded voltage. Neither predicts the
  study controller's commands independently.
- There is one reserved low-charge run per controller for a single vertical
  maneuver. The observed agreement or disagreement is limited to those
  recorded cases. The energy comparison checks consistency between two
  published channels. This is previously inspected DEVELOPMENT evidence.
  The [research registry](../../data/research/registry.json) records the
  qualifications and the [canonical index](../../data/registry.json)
  carries them after reconciliation.

## P0A-014 controller-coupling handoff

1. Separate replay and autonomous runs at their inputs. In replay, label
   `zauxd` and drive channels as conditioning measurements. In an
   autonomous run accept only an exogenous mission reference, the initialized
   plant state, supported configuration, and observations available at or
   before the current clock. Generate the auxiliary reference and drive
   requests in the controller. Keep the logged auxiliary and drive channels
   out of the autonomous input and use them only for comparisons.
2. Use one authoritative plant and clock. Resolve controller demand against
   tick-start battery state and actual branch load. Feed the P0A-006 loaded
   bus through the P0A-012 RPM/thrust ceilings and allocator residuals,
   integrate the **delivered** wrench, and feed the resulting motion and
   electrical state back to the controller at the next declared clock.
   Bound supported actuator dynamics and saturation explicitly. Record
   requested and delivered wrench, branch current and voltage, SOC,
   saturation and controller response on that clock. Use the same model
   and clock for the local desktop worker path.
3. Preserve the bracketed, applicable physical-limit `FAIL` and exact
   branch verdicts. Refuse a trajectory beyond an unsupported collapse,
   protection or recovery mechanism as `INCONCLUSIVE`. Never implement the
   study's 14 V scoring reference as a generic cutoff, or force the 11a
   descent using the recorded crash trace. An autonomous QDrone claim
   requires the controller and protection settings and matching electrical
   and actuator evidence. The historical miss is a comparator, not a
   target to fit in the reserved runs.
4. Verify causal prefixes by changing, reordering or withholding future
   telemetry and checking earlier commands and states stay identical.
   Verify the boundary just above, at and below applicable voltage and
   capability limits, including nominal, sagged and cutout cases.
   Require delivered thrust and saturation changes to alter motion in the
   authoritative loop and preserve strict `FAIL` versus `INCONCLUSIVE`.
   Run `test_p0a_014.py` plus `test_p0a_006.py` and `test_p0a_012.py`.
   Preserve this freeze and compare any later, separately documented
   result against it without relabeling the old conditional replay.
